Why this exists

The case reads like real law. It isn't.

In Mata v. Avianca (S.D.N.Y. 2023), two attorneys filed a brief built on cases a chatbot invented, and were sanctioned for it. A fabricated case isn't a public fact a fact-checker can look up. Only the firm's own cite-check can catch it.

Para is that cite-check, at the moment it matters: between "the AI drafted it" and "the partner relied on it." A paralegal at a five-person firm gets the protection of a dedicated cite-checking team.

How it decides

Four signals. The strictest one wins.

Every memo is checked four ways at once. Para's own code makes the call, never an AI model: agents add evidence and can only make a decision stricter.

1

Your firm's citation lists

Every case, short name and bare reporter cite is matched against the cases your firm has verified and the ones it knows are fake. Wrong volume, year, party or holding is caught.

Deterministic

2

Ezio fact-check

Trust tier, tone, bias and agreement, plus a fact-check of public claims such as what an amendment says. A contradicted claim blocks. Para ships with the buildathon's Ezio simulation; set EZIO_URL to use a live one.

3

AI cite-auditor

Reads the memo like a careful associate: finds authorities in any format and asks whether each paraphrased holding actually matches the case.

4

AI memo reviewer

Flags advocacy that has no place in a research memo: "slam dunk", certainty about outcomes, emotional language, one-sided framing.

Hermes on HarnessRouter

✓ Approve

Every check passes. The memo goes to the partner, and only then.

‖ Hold

A case on neither list, advocacy, a weak score, an unfinished placeholder, or any check that couldn't finish. An attorney logs a reason, then proceeds or rejects.

✕ Block

A fabricated or misstated case, or a claim Ezio contradicts. It can never be pushed through; fix it and resend.

  • Fails closed. If Ezio or an agent is down, slow or unreadable, the memo is held, never sent unread.
  • Agents can't lower a decision. An AI saying "this case is fine" never overrides your list. A memo can't talk its way through.
  • Every call is audited. Input, evidence, reason, reviewer and whether it was sent, in an append-only log.

The agents

Three Hermes agents on HarnessRouter, one job each.

Each agent is a configured harness with its own instructions, tools and limits. They are split by job and permission: the two that judge can't write, and the one that writes never judges.

Questions

What firms ask first

Where does my memo go?

The citation lists, the policy, the review queue and the audit log all run on your computer. To run the AI checks, the memo text and your citation lists are sent to HarnessRouter under your own account. This release scores memos with a simulated Ezio that runs locally; if you point it at a live Ezio endpoint, the memo goes there too. Nothing is sent anywhere else, and Para has no server of its own.

Can an AI model approve a bad memo?

No. Para's own code makes every decision. Agent findings can only make a decision stricter, and a finding is thrown out unless its quote is in the memo word for word and the case it names is really on your list. An agent saying "this case is fine" never overrides your list.

What happens if HarnessRouter or Ezio is down?

The memo is held for an attorney, never sent unread. Your citation lists still block a known-fake or misstated case on their own, even with no key and no network.

Does this replace our cite-checking?

It makes it happen every time, before the partner sees the memo. It checks cases against the lists your firm maintains; a case on neither list is held for a person to verify. Para is not a citator and does not give legal advice.

How do we use our own citation lists?

The lists are two JSON files in firm/: verified cases with citation, year and a one-line holding, and cases known to be fabricated. A pilot replaces the sample lists with yours.

Where is my HarnessRouter key kept?

In ~/.para/harnessrouter.key, readable only by your user account. It is used only by the Para server on your machine; the browser never sees it.

Can I see what an agent did?

Yes. Every agent result in the app has a "View trace" link showing what the agent returned, and every decision is written to an append-only audit log with its evidence.
